Build mode:

Opening setup: each player places a free settlement, then a road touching that settlement. After everyone places once, the order reverses for each player’s second settlement and road. Your second settlement grants resources from adjacent hexes. Choose the matching build mode before clicking a road edge or intersection. During normal play, settlements must connect to your road network and keep at least one empty intersection between settlements.
